# Code of Conduct

> `SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-OmniCompass-Evaluation-1.0`. Copyright (c) 2026 The Omni-Compass LLC.

Everyone who takes part in this repository - issues, discussions, pull requests, pilots - is expected to be
professional, respectful and truthful. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing others' private
information, and misrepresenting results are not tolerated. Results are reported with their evidence class, and no
one may present a simulation as a hardware measurement.

The Omni-Compass LLC maintains this repository and may remove content, close threads, or block participants who do not
follow this code. Report conduct concerns to The Omni-Compass LLC through the contact in `SUPPORT.md`.
